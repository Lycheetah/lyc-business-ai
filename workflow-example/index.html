What this walkthrough proves

Five simple ideas, one fictional job.

    Known and missing details stay separate

    The original enquiry stays visible. Confirmed details are organised, while missing answers are clearly marked.

  2. 02
    Approved rules set the price

    Software calculates the ready total of NZD 4,347.00 from the fictional business pricebook. This is not public LYC pricing.
