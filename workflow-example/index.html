Illustrative synthetic example

Customer enquiry to quote brief

A small flooring business receives enquiries by email or web form. The first useful job is not to invent a price; it is to turn an unstructured enquiry into a complete, reviewable handoff.

Harbour Home Flooring — fictional service business

The example shows the shape of a useful first workflow. It does not assume that another business uses the same process.

01 — What arrives

Start with the messy message, not a perfect form.

Synthetic customer enquiry

“Hi, we’re renovating a home in Mosgiel and need about 42 m² of laminate in the living room and hallway. Could someone measure next Tuesday after 3 pm? There’s old skirting to remove. We haven’t chosen the product yet. — Jamie (synthetic example)”

02 — What gets structured

Make the useful facts and the gaps visible.

Service
Residential flooring enquiry
Area
Approx. 42 m² (customer estimate)
Location
Mosgiel; exact site address not supplied
Timing
Next Tuesday after 3 pm — needs confirmation
Existing work
Old skirting to remove
Product
Not chosen yet

03 — Check before drafting

Missing information is a question, not an invitation to guess.

  • Exact site address and best contact details
  • Product, finish and any preferred brand
  • Subfloor condition and access constraints
  • A confirmed measure window
  • Who has authority to approve the eventual quote

Fictional business rules

  • No final quote before the product and site requirements are confirmed.
  • Never invent price, stock, availability or a booking.
  • Missing or conflicting information becomes a visible question or safe stop.
  • Every customer-facing draft is reviewed and sent by a person.

04 — The governed handoff

The agent prepares work. A person owns the commitment.

01

Structure

Extract the request into named fields while keeping the original source visible.

02

Check

Compare the fields with the business’s required information and boundaries.

03

Draft

Prepare clarifying questions and a clean internal handoff for staff review.

04

Approve

A human edits, approves, sends and records the next action—or stops it.

Draft for staff approval

Hi Jamie — thanks for getting in touch. Before we prepare an estimate, could you confirm the site address, your preferred laminate or finish, and whether access after 3 pm next Tuesday works for a measure? We’ll review the details and come back with the next step. Draft for staff approval — not sent by the agent.

Human checks

  • Verify the extracted details against the original enquiry.
  • Edit and approve the reply before it leaves the business.
  • Confirm site-measure availability and any customer commitment.
  • Own the final quote, price, booking and customer communication.

05 — Prove it before expanding

A pilot measures the workflow, not vanity numbers.

The owner decides whether the workflow is worth continuing after reviewing the evidence and corrections.

Time from enquiry to staff-ready draft
Missing details caught before follow-up
Human corrections per enquiry
Safe stops and escalations
No-price / no-commitment boundary breaches

This is a fictional, synthetic discussion starter. It is not a Carpet Plus workflow, customer result or promise of savings. The real workflow is discovered with the owner before any data or system access is considered.
