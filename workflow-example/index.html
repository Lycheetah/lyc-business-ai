The witness path

One complete fictional loop.

Follow the gold thread from intake to receipt. Gold marks decisions and evidence. Lychee red marks a stop. The same run appears on the homepage demo.

  1. 01
    Messy enquiry enters

    The original customer text stays visible. Nothing is inferred yet.

  2. 02
    Facts and unknowns separate

    Named fields surface. Missing information stays missing — never invented.

  3. 03
    Approved rules are checked

    Owner boundaries decide what may proceed before any price is spoken.

  4. 04
    Deterministic calculation

    Arithmetic runs only against admitted rates. No product code means no material total.

  5. 05
    Safe stop when needed

    Uncertainty pauses the lane. The specialist does not improvise a quote or booking.

  6. 06
    Review-ready draft

    A human-readable follow-up is prepared for inspection. Nothing is sent.

  7. 07
    Human decides

    Edit, request missing detail, hold, or stop. Authority stays with the business.

  8. 08
    Receipt records

    What happened, what remains open, and who owns the next action stay inspectable.
