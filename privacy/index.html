Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

REQUIRES OWNER / LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION.

This is a plain-language scaffold. It must be reviewed and approved by a qualified legal professional before this page goes live.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the LYC Business AI website and the information collected through this website. It does not cover customer systems, customer deployments or data processed through LYC Business AI products — those are governed by separate agreements.

2. What the website collects

When you visit this website, standard web server logs may collect IP addresses, browser type, pages visited and referrer information. This data is used for operational and security purposes.

When you submit a contact form, we collect the information you provide — name, business name, email, industry, workflow description and any other details you include. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

3. Why data is collected

Website logs: operational security, abuse prevention, and server performance monitoring.

Form submissions: to respond to your workflow enquiry and determine whether the described workflow is a sensible fit for AI automation.

4. Where form data is sent and stored

Form submissions are currently processed client-side and are not transmitted to a server. When a real endpoint is configured, form data will be sent to a controlled destination owned by LYC Business AI. You will be notified when this changes.

5. Who can access it

Only authorised LYC Business AI personnel involved in responding to your enquiry. We do not sell, share or provide form data to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Retention and deletion

Form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us through the website contact form.

7. Analytics and cookies

The website currently sets no non-essential cookies. Analytics are optional and configurable. If analytics are added in the future, the smallest privacy-respecting configuration will be used and this policy will be updated to disclose it accurately.

8. Third-party processors

The website is hosted on infrastructure that may use standard content delivery and security services. No form data is sent to third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

9. Submitting an inquiry does not authorise system access

Submitting a contact form does not authorise LYC Business AI to access your systems, create a production agent, or begin processing your business data. Those steps require a separate agreement after workflow discovery and fit confirmation.

10. Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact us through the website contact form.
