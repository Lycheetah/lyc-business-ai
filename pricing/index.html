Frequently asked questions

Does the founding pilot price include GST?

The working offer is NZ$990 setup plus NZ$149 for the first 30 live days. All prices are in New Zealand dollars and are plus GST if applicable. GST registration and final invoice wording will be confirmed before publication.

What does the setup fee cover?

Workflow discovery, business extraction, agent configuration, initial testing, and controlled pilot setup. This is real engineering work, not a click-to-activate product.

What happens after day 30?

We review the evidence together — inquiry completeness, time saved, clarification rate, owner corrections, and whether the workflow actually improved. Only then do we discuss whether to continue, expand or stop.

What if I exceed 100 conversations?

We notify you at 80% of the allowance and pause at the cap unless you approve a separately quoted extension. There are no automatic overage charges.

Can I cancel during the pilot?

There is no long lock-in after the pilot. Continuation is monthly and can be stopped before the next renewal. Exact pilot cancellation, data return, and deletion terms are agreed before work begins.

What data do you collect from my business?

Only what you explicitly provide for the workflow. Data routes, providers, retention, and permissions are reviewed before customer information is admitted. See our Privacy policy.

Does the AI approve quotes or make commitments?

No. The system proposes, drafts, and structures. The owner approves and sends. Human authority is preserved at every consequential step.

What integrations are included?

Email, accounting, CRM, calendar, job management, inventory and document systems can connect through scoped, explicit permissions. Actual connections are configured per workflow during discovery.
