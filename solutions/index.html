Four useful outcomes

Practical work first. Not a chatbot product catalogue.

A specialist is shaped around one repetitive admin friction. Quote and scope preparation is the clear first commercial fit. Broader abilities appear only after that boundary is understood.

  1. 01
    Enquiry triage

    Structure a messy message into facts, unknowns and the next useful question.

  2. 02
    Quote and scope preparation

    Apply approved pricing rules, run deterministic calculations, and prepare a review-ready draft.

  3. 03
    Document preparation

    Fill approved templates and assemble a proposal or handoff for human review.

  4. 04
    Follow-up and internal queues

    Surface open loops and next actions without silently contacting anyone.

How an install starts

A small, reviewable path from workflow to specialist.

The agent can retrieve, structure, ask and draft inside an agreed workflow. A person remains responsible for approval, sending, booking, payment, pricing and other consequential commitments.

    Choose one piece of work

    Name the repetitive workflow and the person who owns it.

  2. 02

    Choose the sources

    Bring only the approved documents, rules, examples and fields needed.

  3. 03

    Choose the boundary

    Decide what the agent may read, ask, structure or draft — and what it must never do.

  4. 04

    Choose the proof

    Agree the baseline, scenarios, correction rate and review date.

    Choose what comes next

    At review, continue, expand or stop. The agent never decides that for you.
