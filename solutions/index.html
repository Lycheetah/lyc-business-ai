A small, reviewable path from workflow to agent

02

Choose the sources

What a business agent is

A business agent is software that uses AI to interpret information, retrieve approved knowledge, apply business rules, and produce useful structured output within a defined workflow.

What LYC Business AI adds

Beyond a generic model, LYC Business AI structures your approved business knowledge, encodes your rules and boundaries, integrates with your existing tools, and preserves human authority for consequential actions.

Business-specific operating knowledge

Your approved services, pricing references, policies, documents, workflows, exclusions, escalation rules, service areas and examples — all structured into an operating specification the agent can reference.

Workflow examples

Trade quoting, dealership enquiry handling, office intake and triage, retail operations knowledge. Each vertical requires its own rules, evidence and evaluation.

Human authority

Important commitments remain under human control. The agent drafts, structures and retrieves — but binding decisions stay with the right person.

Integration

Where possible, keep existing software in place. Email, accounting, CRM, calendar, job management, inventory and document systems can connect through scoped, explicit permissions.

Pilot model

Start with one workflow, prove the value with a controlled pilot, then expand only after evidence supports it.
