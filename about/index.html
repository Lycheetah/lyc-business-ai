About

Independent business systems engineering from New Zealand.

LYC Business AI builds controlled agents and automation around the workflows New Zealand businesses already use.

LYC Business AI is an independent New Zealand commercial systems project focused on practical business workflows.

Customers do not need to adopt a philosophy, a model vendor or a new operating culture. They need a useful system that fits their workflow and produces evidence that it is worth keeping.

The company is building from narrow real-world problems outward: one workflow, one measurable result and one set of clearly defined boundaries at a time.

Useful business AI should not be reserved for enterprise budgets. LYC Business AI uses reusable engineering, fixed scopes and visible starting prices so a New Zealand business can prove one workflow before taking on a large project.

Founder

LYC Business AI was founded by Mackenzie Clark, a self-taught New Zealand AI builder and systems researcher. The work combines practical workflow design, controlled automation, evaluation, and product engineering while keeping business authority with the customer.
