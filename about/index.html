Why this exists

Useful business AI should fit the business, not ask the business to become a lab.

I’m Mackenzie Conor James Clark, a New Zealand sole trader trading as LYC Business AI. I started it because many businesses have the same practical problem: valuable time is lost copying information, checking details, chasing answers and preparing work that still needs a person to approve.

LYC starts with the work a business already does: what arrives, what needs checking, how prices are calculated, and what the finished draft should look like. From there I configure one focused tool and test it against realistic examples.

The goal is simple: save useful time without forcing a business to replace every system it already uses. The owner reviews the work, decides what changes, and chooses whether it is worth continuing.
