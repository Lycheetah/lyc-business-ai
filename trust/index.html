Boundary architecture

Trust is engineered as structure, not a slogan.

Designed against NZ privacy principles

Customer information is handled under New Zealand privacy principles for the specific workflow. Routes, retention and permissions are agreed before private material is admitted. Legal review remains a founder decision for production use.

Private sources stay out of public training

Approved business material is not admitted during the public demonstration. Before any private source is considered, the business and LYC must agree source, purpose, provider route, retention, permissions and reviewer.

Deterministic arithmetic engine

Language models structure language; code calculates dollars. Pricing calculations stay locked to approved rules wherever the work is numerical.

Human authorization gate

Quotes, emails and external communications remain drafts until explicitly authorized by the designated business owner.

Controlled pilot mode

Prepare-for-review. Period.

The specialist may draft. Binding commitments stay with the business. Expansion beyond prepare-for-review is a separate, written decision after evidence review.

When the answer is uncertain

What we do when the answer is uncertain

The customer did not provide enough detail

The agent asks specific clarification questions before proceeding. It does not assume, fill gaps with plausible text or present an incomplete scope as complete.

The approved knowledge base does not cover the situation

The agent says so explicitly and escalates to the business owner. It does not improvise a rule or retrieve unapproved information.

The calculation depends on unknown variables

The agent flags the unknowns, shows what is verified and what is not, and presents the draft as preliminary — not final.

The request falls outside the configured workflow

The agent stops and says it cannot help with that request within its current permissions. It does not attempt to operate outside its boundaries.
