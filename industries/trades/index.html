Industry

Built for trade businesses that quote and scope real jobs.

Trade businesses carry valuable knowledge in people's heads — pricing rules, site conditions, material preferences, supplier relationships, and the difference between a straightforward job and one that needs a site visit. QuoteForge is being built to capture and structure that knowledge into a repeatable, measurable workflow.

Typical workflow friction

Incomplete customer messages with missing details
Quoting from memory or old spreadsheets

Evenings spent rebuilding similar quotes

What AI may do

  • Structure incoming enquiries into clear scopes
  • Identify missing information and ask relevant questions
  • Retrieve approved pricing and business rules
  • Prepare preliminary estimates for owner review

What remains human

  • Final quote approval
  • Pricing decisions
  • Site inspections
  • Customer commitments
  • Exception handling
