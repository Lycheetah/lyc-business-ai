Industry

Structured enquiry handling for dealerships.

Vehicle dealerships handle high volumes of customer enquiries across sales, trade-ins, servicing and parts. Each pathway has different rules, required information and handoff points. DealerForge is in commercial research to understand how AI agents can structure and accelerate these workflows while keeping sales authority with the right person.

ResearchCommercial research / planned specialist

Typical workflow friction

High-volume enquiries with varied quality
Incomplete customer requirements
Manual CRM data entry
Inconsistent follow-up processes
Information scattered across channels

What AI may do

  • Capture and structure customer requirements
  • Route enquiries to the right salesperson
  • Prepare handoff summaries with context
  • Track follow-up status

What remains human

  • Pricing decisions
  • Trade valuations
  • Finance arrangements
  • Customer relationships
  • Deal commitments

Current status

Vehicle Dealerships workflows are in commercial research. Not currently offered as a production product.
