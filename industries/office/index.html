Industry

Structured intake for professional service firms.

Project-based professional firms manage complex intake processes — gathering requirements, qualifying opportunities, preparing proposals and coordinating handoffs. OpsForge is in commercial research to understand how AI agents can structure these workflows while preserving professional judgement.

ResearchCommercial research / planned specialist

Typical workflow friction

Complex intake requirements across multiple stakeholders
Inconsistent information gathering
Time spent on repetitive proposal preparation
Handoff gaps between team members
Follow-up tasks falling through cracks

What AI may do

  • Structure intake questionnaires based on service type
  • Gather and organize required information
  • Prepare first-draft documents from templates
  • Track completion and escalation status

What remains human

  • Scope definition
  • Pricing and engagement terms
  • Quality review
  • Client relationships
  • Professional sign-off

Current status

Office / Professional workflows are in commercial research. Not currently offered as a production product.
