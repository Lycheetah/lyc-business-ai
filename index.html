Why LYC is different

AI is easy to access. Useful business automation is harder.

A general chatbot does not know your pricing, margin thresholds, warranties, or when to stop and alert a human.

Generic chat

Writes plausible prose from general training. Can invent rates and make unauthorized promises.

Fixed automation

Follows rigid paths. Breaks when the enquiry is messy or incomplete.

LYC specialist

Reads the enquiry, checks the required details, uses your rules, and prepares a draft you can review.

What QuoteForge is built to improve

Less rebuilding. Better questions. A cleaner decision.

The first pilot tests whether these improvements hold inside one real business workflow. They are targets, not customer results.
