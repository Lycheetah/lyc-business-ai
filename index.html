How it works

Start with the workflow, not the AI.

1

Pick one painful workflow

Choose the single workflow that wastes the most time or costs the most money. We start narrow and prove value before expanding.

2

Teach us the business

Provide your approved rules, pricing, documents, examples and exclusions. We structure these into an operating specification you can review.

3

Build and test

We configure the agent, connect approved sources, set permissions and run realistic scenarios against your business rules.

4

Run a controlled pilot

Use it on real enquiries with human oversight. Measure corrections, time saved, failures and whether the workflow actually improved.

Clear limits are a feature.

Every workflow gets explicit boundaries. Here is an example of how permissions work.

Capability
Default

e.g. Retrieve this week's approved timber pricing from your price list

✓Allowed
