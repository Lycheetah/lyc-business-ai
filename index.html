Why LYC is different

AI is easy to access. Useful business automation is harder.

A general chatbot does not know your pricing, margin thresholds, warranties, or when to stop and alert a human.

Generic chat

Writes plausible prose from general training. Can invent rates and make unauthorized promises.

Fixed automation

Follows rigid paths. Breaks when the enquiry is messy or incomplete.

LYC specialist
