Safe stop

If something is missing, we pause — not improvise.

If inputs, authority, evidence, or fit are missing, the process pauses rather than improvising. The system does not fill gaps with plausible text, invent pricing, or operate outside its configured permissions.

This is a feature, not a limitation. The pause protects your business from incorrect assumptions and unauthorised actions.
